# AI Literacy and Ethics: Foundations

A self-paced introduction to AI for all Uplift staff. It covers what AI is and how it works, how to protect scholar data by choosing the right tool, how to write better prompts, and why Uplift scholars need AI-literate adults.

| **Audience** | All staff: teachers, campus leaders and central office |
| **Length** | About 25 minutes, self-paced. Learners can stop and resume. |

## What learners do

The module is 41 short slides. Learners move with **Previous** and **Next**, or jump back to any slide they've already seen from the **Menu** (it folds away, and on phones it opens over the slide). The top bar has two dropdowns: **Resources** (links to the AI Policy, approved AI tools, the AI Guidebook, sample prompts and ai@uplifteducation.org) and **Settings** (Sound effects, off by default, which also turns on the sounds in the animations; and Viewing tips: full screen and animation steps for PC and Mac, shown on the Welcome slide by default). Slides they haven't reached yet are locked. A "Pre-flight check" slide on a launch pad lists the three things learners will be able to do: they check each one off, then a countdown runs, the rocket flies off the top of the screen and the module moves on to Section 1 by itself (the slide says so before they start). The same slide says what it takes to finish. Sections 1–4 each open with a short Section start slide where a rocket flies on to that section's planet. On slides with an activity, **Next** stays locked until it's done: checking off the pre-flight check, the Mission 1 scan, the "Sounds right, can be wrong" sorting game, approving the email on the Agentic AI slide, selecting every part of the worked-example prompt, each practice scenario (one per slide, for the learner's role), each section-check question, and passing each section check.

1. **AI basics:** the three levels of AI (predictive, generative and agentic), and hallucinations and bias. Ends with a 3-question check (2 of 3 to pass)
2. **Responsible AI:** the four questions (safe, fair, honest, human deciding), privacy, account type, Claude for Educators (what it is, how to sign up, and how to merge an existing Claude account) and why Uplift. Ends with a 3-question check (2 of 3 to pass)
3. **Prompt writing:** the CRAFT method, a worked example and sample prompts for your role. Ends with a 2-question check (1 of 2 to pass)
4. **Practice:** "Space mission: Detect the false signal". Learners pick their role (Teacher, Campus staff or Central office, the same picker as the worked example) and answer three scenarios for it, one per slide. In each, two signals are accurate and one is false. Not scored: answering each scenario unlocks Next.
5. **Before you go:** key takeaways and a short reflection (nothing typed is saved)

Each section check has one multiple-choice question per slide. **Check my answer** marks the correct answer (and the
learner's choice if it was wrong), with no explanation. The last question shows the check's result. A learner who doesn't reach the pass mark selects
**Try again**, which clears that check's answers and goes back to its first question. Next stays locked until the
check is passed.

| Check | Questions (old knowledge check number) |
|---|---|
| AI basics | Example of generative AI (Q1), what a hallucination is (Q2), example of agentic AI (Q6) |
| Responsible AI | Which tool for an IEP summary (Q3), what's OK on a personal account (Q4), a vendor AI that rejects applicants (Q7) |
| Prompt writing | Which request is de-identified (Q5), how to improve "Help me with grading" (Q8) |

All names and scenarios are made up. The module has no student photos and no video. Its only audio is three short sound effects (click, right, wrong). They're off by default (from v3.1), and learners can turn them on under **Settings** in the top bar.

## How completion and scoring work

| LMS field | What sets it |
|---|---|
| `cmi.completion_status` | **completed** once the learner has reached every section (Welcome and sections 1–5) and passed all three section checks. A section counts once its last slide is shown, or, for sections 1–3, once its check is passed and the learner moves on to the next section. The menu ticks a section at the same moment. |
| `cmi.success_status` | **passed** once all three section checks are passed. The module never sets **failed**: a learner retries a check until they pass it. |
| `cmi.score.raw` / `cmi.score.scaled` | Correct answers out of 8, counting each check's passing attempt / the same from 0 to 1. For information only: passing is decided by the checks, so a learner can pass with 5 of 8 (2 + 2 + 1). |
| `cmi.progress_measure` | Share of milestones reached so far: the six sections plus the three checks |
| `cmi.suspend_data` | Which sections the learner has reached and which checks they've passed. A returning learner picks up at the first section they haven't reached, or at the first check they haven't passed. |

- Each check's pass mark is in the module (`data-pass` on its questions), not in the manifest. From v2.1 the package is built **without** a mastery score, so the manifest has no `minNormalizedMeasure` and the LMS never turns a score into passed or failed. The module sets **passed** itself.
- A learner who passed on an earlier visit (including the v2.0 knowledge check) keeps their pass and score. Their checks count as passed, and answering them again changes nothing in the LMS.
- Learners from v2.0 or earlier who hadn't passed are sent to the first check they haven't passed when they come back.
- The **completion** setting is `completionThreshold minProgressMeasure="1.0"` (every milestone must be reached). Check it after packaging (see step 3 below).

## How to rebuild the package

1. Make sure `assets/ceo-welcome.webm` is in the module folder (it isn't in git; see the table above). Without it the Welcome slide shows an empty video.
2. Edit `sources/00 - Foundations AI Literacy and Ethics/index.html`. For quick testing, set `var DEV = true;` at the top of the script: every slide opens from the menu, Next is never locked and animations skip to their end, with a "Dev mode" badge in the top bar. **Set it back to `false` before packaging.**
3. Package it with the `scorm` tool (`scorm_package`), using:
   `title` "AI Literacy and Ethics: Foundations", `identifier` `uplift-ai-foundations`, `language` `en-US`,
   `scorm_version` `2004`, `auto_milestones` `false`, and **no** `mastery_score` (from v2.1; v2.0 and earlier used `0.8`).
4. **Check the completion threshold.** In `imsmanifest.xml` inside the zip, `<adlcp:completionThreshold>` must have
   `minProgressMeasure="1.0"`, so learners are complete only once every milestone is reached. Change it to `1.0` if the
   tool wrote anything lower. Also check there's no `<imsss:minNormalizedMeasure>`.
   Without a mastery score the tool writes **no** sequencing at all, so add these inside `<item>` after its `<title>`
   (and declare `xmlns:imsss="http://www.imsglobal.org/xsd/imsss"` plus `imsss_v1p0.xsd` in `xsi:schemaLocation`), as v2.1 does:
   `<adlcp:completionThreshold completedByMeasure="true" minProgressMeasure="1.0"/>` and
   `<imsss:sequencing><imsss:deliveryControls completionSetByContent="true" objectiveSetByContent="true"/></imsss:sequencing>`.
5. Run `scorm_validate`, then test in SCORM Cloud or the LMS sandbox (see the root README's testing checklist).
6. Rename to `foundations-v<major>.<minor>-scorm.zip`, commit it next to the earlier versions and add a row below.

Keep the `identifier` the same in every version so the LMS can **replace** the package instead of treating it as a new course.
