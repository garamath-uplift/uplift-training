For the best experience, make this module full screen and make sure animation effects are turned on. Follow the steps for your or .

On a PC (Windows 11)

  1. Full screen: press F11. On some laptops, press Fn + F11. Press it again to leave full screen.
  2. Animation effects: open Start > Settings > Accessibility > Visual effects, and turn Animation effects on.

On a Mac

  1. Full screen: press Control + Command + F, or select the green button at the top left of the browser window. Do it again to leave full screen.
  2. Animation effects: open the Apple menu > System Settings > Accessibility > Display, and turn Reduce motion off.
